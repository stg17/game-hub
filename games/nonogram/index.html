How to read a plate

  1. 2 3 beside a row means a run of two, then a gap, then a run of three — in that order, with at least one blank square between them.
  2. 0 means the line carries no ink at all.
  3. Click a square to ink it. Click again to cross it off as blank, and once more to clear it. Drag to do a whole run at once.
  4. Crossing off the blanks is only for your own bookkeeping — the plate is finished when the ink is right, whether or not you marked the rest.

Keyboard: arrow keys move, Space inks, X crosses off, H asks for one deduction, Esc goes back.

One instruction is printed large. The rest is printed small, and it contradicts the large one. Click the right shape before the clock runs out.

The agreement

  1. The headline says click the biggest shape. That is the instruction, until a clause says otherwise.
  2. Clauses are numbered. Any clause whose condition is met replaces the instruction.
  3. A later clause overrides an earlier one. When two apply, the one further down the page wins.
  4. A new clause is added every third correct answer, and the clock gets shorter. One wrong answer ends the run.

Every shape is labelled with its colour and its number, so you can play this by reading and by keyboard: press 1 – 6 to choose.

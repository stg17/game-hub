How to play
  • Read the big line. It tells you which shape to click.
  • Then read the numbered rules under it. A rule can change the answer.
  • If more than one rule applies, use the last one in the list.
  • Every 3 correct answers, a new rule is added to the list.
  • You get less time to answer as the game goes on.
  • One wrong click, or running out of time, ends the game.
  • Your score is the most correct answers you got in a row.
  • You can press 1–6 to pick a shape instead of clicking.
